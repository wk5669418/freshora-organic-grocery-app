FRESHORA — Organic Grocery Delivery App


 Project Overview

Freshora is a mobile-first organic grocery delivery app designed to make fresh, certified organic products easy to discover, evaluate, and order.

The design focuses on a clean, trustworthy, and premium shopping experience with strong visual hierarchy, simple navigation, clear product information, and a streamlined checkout flow.

  Design Goals

 Create a clean and modern organic grocery experience
 Make product discovery fast and intuitive
 Build trust through clear organic-quality information
 Simplify cart and checkout interactions
 Maintain a consistent visual design system across the app
 Provide a smooth end-to-end ordering journey

 Key Screens

1. Splash Screen — Freshora brand introduction
2. Home — Product discovery and fresh recommendations
3. Product Details — Product image, price, quality information, quantity and add-to-cart action
4. Search — Product search, recent searches and popular searches
5. Category — Organized browsing across grocery categories
6. Cart — Selected products, quantities and order summary
7. Checkout — Delivery address, delivery method and payment method
8. Order Confirmed — Successful order confirmation and order details

 Key Features

 Organic product discovery
 Search and category browsing
 Product recommendations
 Product quality and certification indicators
 Quantity selection
 Shopping cart
 Delivery address management
 Multiple delivery/payment options
 Order summary
 Order confirmation flow

 UI/UX Design Direction

Style: Clean, minimal, premium and nature-inspired
Visual Language: Organic, fresh and trustworthy
Typography: Modern and highly readable
Layout: Mobile-first with clear spacing and visual hierarchy
Components: Consistent cards, buttons, navigation and product elements
UX Focus: Simple navigation, reduced friction and clear calls to action
